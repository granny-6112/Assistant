Welcome to Assistant

Assistant answers questions about your screen. To do that it needs a free Gemini key from Google. It takes about a minute.

  1. Get your free key. Sign in with any Google account, click Create API key, then copy it.

  2. Paste it here.

  3. Allow the microphone (optional), so you can ask out loud with the mic button.

You're all set. Push your mouse to the top of the screen (or press the Copilot key) whenever you want Assistant. Try asking "what's on my screen?".
